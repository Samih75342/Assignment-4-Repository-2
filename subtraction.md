Subtraction is easy.
Instead of jumping forwards like you do with addition, you will jump backwards.
You are taking away the second number from the first number, finding the different between the two.
Let's start with a single digit example.
Given 8 - 4, lets find the answer.
Let's start at 8 and make four jumps backwards.
8, 7 (first jump), 6 (second jump), 5 (third jump), and 4 our fourth and final jump.
So we found that 8 - 4 = 4
